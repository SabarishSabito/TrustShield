About TrustShield

TrustShield is an automated multi-modal forensic platform engineered to detect fake profiles, automated bot accounts, and Sybil clusters across Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Facebook.

Instead of relying on single metrics like follower counts, TrustShield cross-examines accounts using a real-time 4-pillar forensic ensemble:

Behavioral Velocity Measures media posting frequency, direct messaging cadences, and active lifespan to flag non-human activity bursts.
Linguistic NLP Evaluates handle character entropy, promotional keyword patterns, and off-platform phishing redirects (Telegram, WhatsApp, Bitly).
Vision & OCR Forensics Features client-side neural OCR that reads screenshots directly to extract account data and inspect profile pictures for synthetic GAN artifacts.
Network Topology Flags asymmetric follow-churn anomalies and zero-mutual connection patterns across professional and social graphs.
Every audit generates a transparent, multi-axis risk score (0% to 100%), visualizes the threat vectors on a dynamic radar chart, and logs all forensic findings to an immutable SQL registry for moderation and compliance reporting.
