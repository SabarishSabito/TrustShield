How Trust Shield works

Three clear steps to audit suspicious profiles and understand digital risk signals.

01

Provide Profile Context

Enter a supported public profile URL (Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook) or upload a profile screenshot for automatic OCR extraction.

02

Cross-Examine Signals

The engine computes linguistic entropy, checks account cadences, evaluates image artifacts, and measures reciprocal network distribution.

03

Review Findings

Examine the comprehensive dossier detailing safety rating, specific risk indicators, radar distribution, and actionable verification steps.

Responsible AI & Transparency Commitment

Trust Shield evaluates signals in publicly accessible profile data. A risk indication reflects unusual patterns or missing benchmarks, not proof of malicious intent. Results may be incomplete for private accounts. Users should always exercise independent judgment and verify critical details through official channels.
